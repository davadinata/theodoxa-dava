# Starting Something New: Why I Built This Website


### The Beginning of a Digital Home

Starting this website feels like opening a new chapter in my developer journey. For a while, I've been wanting a space that truly represents my growth, projects, and the things I'm learning along the way—more than what a typical social media profile can capture.

## Why Hugo?

I chose **Hugo** as the foundation for this site after exploring several options. Here's why it stood out:

- **⚡ Blazing Fast**: As a static site generator, Hugo creates incredibly fast-loading pages
- **🛠️ Developer-Friendly**: Written in Go, with a straightforward structure and great documentation
- **🎨 Flexible Theming**: The LoveIt theme provided a beautiful starting point that I could customize
- **📝 Markdown Simplicity**: Writing content in Markdown feels natural and efficient

The process of setting up Hugo reminded me why I love web development—there's something satisfying about seeing your vision come to life through code.

## What to Expect Here

This isn't going to be a formal blog with rigid structures. Think of it more as:

- **A learning journal** where I document technical challenges and solutions
- **A project showcase** for my experiments and serious work alike  
- **A thinking space** for ideas about technology and development
- **A progress tracker** for my journey from junior to (hopefully) senior developer

I believe in learning in public and sharing the process, not just the polished results.
